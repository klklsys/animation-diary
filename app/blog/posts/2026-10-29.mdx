What I enjoy most is turning ideas into engaging visual content that connects with people.

Right now, I mainly work on client projects, and I really enjoy working with different industries.
But once a project is finished, I don't always get to see how it performs. So I don't have many opportunities to improve my work based on the results.
That's why I'm looking for a role where I can be involved in a brand's long-term growth.
I'm interested in BeReal because it's a social media product with a unique concept, and I think there's a lot of creative potential in the Japanese market.
I'd love to use my experience in design and marketing to create content, learn from the results, and keep improving it.


At TikTok for Business, I worked closely with the marketing team on campaigns for major holidays, like Christmas and Valentine's Day.
For most of these campaigns, we created packages for clients, including creator videos, in-app landing pages, branded effects, and other digital content.
I was responsible for designing and producing digital creative assets, especially web design and motion graphics.
One example is a campaign for Disney's movie Wish. It received over 190,000 page views and more than 1,900 user posts.
Through these projects, I gained experience in creating marketing content for different clients and encouraging user engagement.


How do you approach a new creative project?

After receiving a brief, I usually have a meeting with the team to clarify the project goals and what we want to achieve.
Then I create a mood board and develop a few rough visual concepts to explore different directions.
After discussing the ideas with the team, we choose the most suitable direction and move into production.
During production, I keep refining the design based on feedback until it's ready to launch.
I think it's important to understand the purpose of a project before starting the visual design.
